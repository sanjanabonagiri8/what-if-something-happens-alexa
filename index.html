Accessibility Preferences

Choose how you prefer to communicate and receive information. You can change these choices at any time.

Choose the option that best fits you. This saves your preference; it does not turn on a screen reader or connect assistive hardware. You can still use any available option on the emergency screen.

Screen readers must be enabled in your device settings. Braille displays, switches, and wearables need compatible hardware and software. This prototype records your choices but does not directly control those devices.
